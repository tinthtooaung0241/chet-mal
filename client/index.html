    Kitchen log

      Controls

      • WS / ↑↓ — walk, AD — step sideways
      • Mouse (click the view first) or ←→ — look / turn
      • V — switch first-person / overhead view
      • Space / E — pick up, put down, plate, serve
      • Hold F — chop on a cutting board
      • Q — describe what's around you
      • Z — sniff what you hold (spoiled food smells off)
      • H — take a teammate's hand to lead them
      • P / Esc — pause
      • 0–3 — switch chef (all senses, blind, deaf, mute)

      How to cook

      • Take food from a crate, chop it on a board, cook it on a stove.
      • Orders arrive as printed tickets (seen) or phone calls (heard once).
      • Cooked food burns if you leave it. When the sizzle whistles, take it off for a perfect bonus.
      • Grab a plate, put the finished food on it, take it to the pass.
