        Mute chef

        G ping · B bell

        Hold T + 1–6 signs

        1. Take food from a crate
        2. Chop it on a board
        3. Fry it, or three veg in the pot
        4. Plate it (soup goes in a bowl)
        5. Season it at a spice rack, as the ticket says
        6. Serve at the pass

        Meat, fish, eggs, cheese, lettuce and mushrooms live in the fridges: left out too long, they go off. Tickets are seen, phone orders are heard once. When a sizzle whistles, take it off for a perfect bonus.
